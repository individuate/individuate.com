## Bought from this site

**Thirty days, for any reason.** Write to [{{ site.support }}]({{ site.support | mailto('Refund') }}) within 30 days of buying, from the email address you bought with, and say which app. You'll get the full amount back, the same way you paid. There's no form to fill in. If you're willing to say what went wrong, that helps the next person.

**How long it takes.** Individuate sends the refund within five business days. Your bank or card may take up to ten more to show it.

**What happens to the license.** A refund ends the license that came with the purchase.

**After 30 days.** Still write if the app doesn't do what its page says. Individuate will try to fix it, and will refund you if it can't.

## Bought from the App Store

Apple sells and refunds everything bought through the App Store and Mac App Store, including in-app purchases such as Affirmable's. Ask at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Individuate isn't able to issue those refunds itself, but write to support if something isn't working and it can be put right.

## Anthropic credits

Some apps work with Claude through your own Anthropic account. Those credits are bought from Anthropic, not from Individuate, so refunding the app doesn't refund them. They stay in your Anthropic account.

Nothing here reduces any right the law gives you where you live.
