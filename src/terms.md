## The short version

When you buy an Individuate app, you pay once and the app is yours to use. There's no subscription and no account. If it isn't right for you, you can have your money back within 30 days. The [refund policy](/refunds/) says how.

These terms cover apps made by Individuate LLC. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) covers what happens to your information.

## Where you buy

**From the App Store.** Apps and in-app purchases bought through Apple's App Store or Mac App Store are sold by Apple, under Apple's terms and its [standard license agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/). Apple takes the payment, sends the receipt, and handles refunds.

**From this site.** Apps bought from individuate.com are sold through Individuate's payment partner, which acts as the seller of record. It takes the payment, adds any sales tax or VAT your location requires, and sends your receipt. Its name may be the one on your card statement.

## What you get

**A license to use the app.** One purchase lets you use the app on every Mac you use yourself, and lets the people in your household use it on theirs. The app remains Individuate's; what you're buying is the right to use it. Please don't resell it, share your license outside your household, or pass copies around.

{# REVIEW: household use is Claude's suggestion, to match Affirmable's Family Sharing. Decide: one person, a household, or a number of Macs. #}

**No subscription.** You pay once. Nothing renews.

**Updates.** Updates to the version you bought are included. If a later major version is ever sold separately, the one you have keeps working.

**Your license, by email.** After you pay, your license arrives by email. Keep that message. If you lose it, write to [{{ site.support }}]({{ site.support | mailto('License') }}) from the address you bought with and it will be sent again. The app checks the license on your own Mac. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

## What isn't included

**AI services you connect yourself.** Some apps, such as Love Your Food Plan, work with Claude through your own Anthropic account. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use, under your agreement with them. That cost isn't part of the app's price. Individuate takes no share of it and can't refund it.

## Prices and tax

Prices are shown in US dollars, before tax. Tax depends on where you live and is added at checkout. A price may change, but a change never affects something you've already bought.

## What to rely on, and what not to

**Check what matters to your health.** Meal plans, recipes, and nutrition figures in Love Your Food Plan are generated or estimated, and they can be wrong, including about allergens. If a restriction matters to your health, read every ingredient yourself. Nothing in any Individuate app is medical advice.

**As is.** Individuate works to make each app do what its page says, and fixes what it can when something breaks. Beyond that and the refund policy, the apps are provided as they are, without warranties, as far as the law allows. Individuate's liability for any claim about an app is limited to what you paid for it. Some places don't allow limits like these. Where they don't, your rights under local law stand.
